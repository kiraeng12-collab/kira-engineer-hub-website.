## 2. Shot List (what to film)

**A-roll (founder to camera):**
1. Hook line — tight, eye-level, plain background.
2. "We built something different…" — medium shot, movement ok.
3. Closing CTA — confident, slight smile, direct.

**B-roll to capture / screen-record:**
- Screen recording: the AI Daily Desk output, an academy lesson, the VIP chat live.
- Charts on a monitor (marked up with lines/zones).
- Hands on keyboard / phone; coffee-and-charts desk setup.
- A member's-eye view: scrolling the Telegram community.
- City/night ambience for the "alone at night" beat (or stock).

**Gear/tone:** shoot in 4K if possible, natural light or a soft key light, phone
on a tripod is fine. Keep cuts fast (1.5–2.5s). Add captions. Music: calm,
building, "focused" — not aggressive trap/hype.
